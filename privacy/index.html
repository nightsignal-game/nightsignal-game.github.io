In short

  • No account. We do not collect any personal information.
  • Game progress, endings and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no access to the microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Content updates

The app may download new story content and fixes through Expo's update service. Expo's servers receive standard connection information (such as IP address, device and OS version, and app version) and process it under Expo's policies; we receive no information that identifies you.

Content notice

The stories contain horror, stalking, fraud and death themes and are not suitable for young children. The app is not directed to children under 13.
