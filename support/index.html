常見問題

選擇有倒數，來不及怎麼辦？
到「設定 › 限時選擇」改成「寬鬆」或「關閉」。

怎麼看我解鎖了哪些結局？
遊戲中右上角打開「案件檔案」，在「結局」分頁查看。

怎麼重玩？
遊戲選單 › 重新開始本章，或在結局畫面按「再玩一次」。

購買與鑰匙

哪些案件免費？
前兩案免費。其他案件用 2 把鑰匙開啟，開啟後永久保留。每天會自動補 2 把免費鑰匙，剛好開一個案件。

換手機或重新安裝後，買斷不見了？
到「設定 › 購買」按「恢復購買」，用同一個 Apple 帳號就能找回「買斷全部故事」。

鑰匙包可以恢復嗎？
鑰匙包是消耗性項目，鑰匙存在這支手機上，刪除 App 後無法恢復。購買的鑰匙不會過期。

想退款？
所有購買都由 Apple 處理，請到 reportaproblem.apple.com 申請。

FAQ

A choice has a countdown and I can't keep up.
Settings › Timed choices › Relaxed or Off.

Where can I see the endings I've found?
Open the Case File (top right) during a case and check the Endings tab.

How do I replay?
Menu › Restart chapter, or "Play again" on the ending screen.

Purchases and keys

Which cases are free?
The first two cases are free. Every other case opens with 2 keys and stays open. You get 2 free keys every day, enough for one case.

I bought "All Stories" but it is gone on my new iPhone.
Settings › Purchases › Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account.

Can key packs be restored?
Key packs are consumable. Keys are stored on the device and cannot be restored after the app is deleted. Purchased keys never expire.

Refunds
All purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
