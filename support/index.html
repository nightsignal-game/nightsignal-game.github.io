常見問題

選擇有倒數，來不及怎麼辦？
到「設定 › 限時選擇」改成「寬鬆」或「關閉」。

怎麼看我解鎖了哪些結局？
遊戲中右上角打開「案件檔案」，在「結局」分頁查看。

怎麼重玩？
遊戲選單 › 重新開始本章，或在結局畫面按「再玩一次」。

Contact

Questions or ideas: pericprew@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the case number.

FAQ

A choice has a countdown and I can't keep up.
Settings › Timed choices › Relaxed or Off.

Where can I see the endings I've found?
Open the Case File (top right) during a case and check the Endings tab.

How do I replay?
Menu › Restart chapter, or "Play again" on the ending screen.
